# Interactive explorer validation, 4 November 2026

The explorer's population is the 108 disclosure records, linked to 63 restricted items and 47 traditions. The source snapshot is generated by the existing canonical builder. No source record, practice date, present-day status or authenticity assessment is changed by the website.

Country associations follow the canonical tradition region and the documented `site/geography.json` mapping. Broad regional and historical territory is not expanded into guessed country memberships. There are 25 assigned country codes. Cross-border records can count under more than one country; the selected disclosure population remains distinct by ID.

The timeline uses disclosure years and retains their precision. Six undated records remain available in the list, with an explicit inclusion control. Nearby dated records may be clustered for readability, with a visible date-span line and individual dates in the selection dialog. Cluster zoom and date brushing update the same population used by the map, list and CSV export. Historical-context filters distinguish tradition origins and source publication dates; neither stands in for the age or current status of a rite.

## Checks performed

- Canonical equality, snapshot preservation, origin-date interpretation, conservative geography and retained evidence qualifications: four Python checks.
- Frontend joins, intersecting filters, cross-border grain, inclusive dates, missing dates, diacritic-insensitive search, export scope/formula escaping, date sorting and clustering: eight Node test cases.
- Existing canonical, export and OSINT checks retained.
- Local browser at desktop size: verified all 108 disclosures load, Australia selects six disclosures, and the historical-origin plus leak filters return three LDS records. The record dialog preserves the distinction between uploader assertions, unassessed authenticity and undated practice status.
- Dragged the timeline from 1884 to 1957: 27 dated and six undated records remained. The actual browser CSV download parsed as the same 33 records with the expected scope and qualification columns.
- Mobile at 390 × 844: inspected the opening layout and verified the document width equals the viewport width. The timeline has its own horizontal scrolling area; it does not widen the document. Restored the default viewport after the check.
- Filter selections use URL query parameters and survive a browser reload; navigation anchors remain independent of filter state. Browser assets carry content-derived version strings to prevent stale modules mixing with a new page.

The site is a view of a seed catalogue. Its record counts do not measure the prevalence, secrecy or character of any country or tradition. Local build and interaction verification does not by itself establish a production deployment; live-host verification is recorded when completed.
