# Scope audit of the eight Indigenous additions

Audit date: 4 November 2026. Question: does the inspected source establish secrecy, a transmission restriction or initiation for each added item?

The audit rechecked the captured passages and source descriptions used in the expansion. The initial audit found an initiation or restriction basis for all eight, but the subsequent documentary review retained only seven as restricted-item records. The Hee Biki overview remains a contextual source. Contextual claims are attached to the particular rite or knowledge item; they are not separate secrets.

| Item | Explicit basis in the inspected source | Scope decision and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **ITEM-0049, Cherokee notebooks** | [Mooney](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/24788/24788-h/24788-h.htm), p. 310: manuscripts guarded from laity and rivals; recitations deliberately inaudible to clients. **CLM-0174** | Direct evidence of guarded knowledge. The hostile formulas and their interpretation belong to that documented manuscript context. No claim that all Cherokee knowledge is confidential. |
| **ITEM-0050, Hako secret division** | [Fletcher/Murie/Tahirussawichi](https://archive.org/details/hakopawneecer00fletrich), pp. 183, 213 and 339: expressly secret ceremonies, restricted attendance and physical screens. **CLM-0180–0181, 0184** | Direct evidence of a private division, distinct from public portions. This need not be a puberty initiation to fall within scope. |
| **ITEM-0051, Osage transfer rights** | [La Flesche](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/49326/pg49326-images.html), p. 44: a knowledgeable speaker refuses transmission because he lacks the relevant rights; another transfers his own recitation for a fee. **CLM-0186–0187** | Restricted transmission is documented. Payment alone does not prove secrecy. Robe instruction is associated ritual context, with no confidentiality rule established for that instruction itself. |
| **ITEM-0052, Hain initiation** | [Chapman’s inspected second edition](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0043450.pdf), p. 214: a novice learns the masked figure is human and is threatened against revealing the secret. **CLM-0191–0192** | Direct evidence of initiation and guarded knowledge in the historical account. The [companion notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/FW04179.pdf) assign some singing to mothers; that performance role does not itself establish confidentiality of the songs. |
| **ITEM-0053, Barasana He House** | [Bonnemère](https://publishing.cdlib.org/ucpressebooks/public/book/gender-in-amazonia-and-melanesia-an-exploration-of-the-comparative-method.html), pp. 33–35: first viewing by initiates, concealed instruments, a day of exclusion and subsequent seclusion. **CLM-0198–0201** | Explicit initiation and controlled access. Narrowed the exclusion claim to the particular rule described in the passage. The chapter is dependent scholarship; the original fieldwork volume remains uninspected. |
| **ITEM-0054, angakkuq training** | [Rasmussen](https://archive.org/details/intellectualcult00rasm), p. 112: the pupil is expressly described as initiated by a tutor; tuition occurs behind a curtain. **CLM-0204** | Explicit initiation and private ritual instruction. Neither the specialist’s title nor a tuition gift alone establishes secrecy of all knowledge. Alternative training paths remain recorded. |
| **ITEM-0055, Arrernte names and objects** | [Spencer and Gillen](https://sacred-texts.com/book/the-native-tribes-of-central-australia/chapter-v-the-churinga-or-bull-roarers-of-the-arunta-and-other-tribes), pp. 139–140: secret names known only to specified initiated men and controlled storehouse admission. **CLM-0211–0212** | Direct evidence of restricted knowledge and access in that historical local account. Present-day applicability remains unknown. |
| **ITEM-0056, Hee Biki summary** | [UNESCO’s expanded upload description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CqWoosEGy2Q): male children learn during a passage into adulthood. **CLM-0214** | Final decision: moved to a contextual discovery lead. SRC-0109 and ARTF-0017 remain, while ITEM-0056, TRAD-0043, DISC-0099 and CLM-0214–0215 are withdrawn from the unpublished batch. No specific secret content was established. |

## Corrections made

- Put each item’s scope basis into its canonical notes, so it travels with exported records.
- Narrowed the Osage entry to transmission rights; identified paid robe instruction as associated context.
- Focused the Hain title and access rule on the initiatory secret. Removed the implication that exclusive singing roles establish confidential transmission.
- Narrowed the Barasana claim to the source’s stated first viewing and specific day of exclusion. Made Rasmussen’s explicit initiation wording visible in its claim.
- Distinguished purposes of rites from reasons for secrecy in the relevant `stated_reason` fields.
- Made the public Hee Biki summary’s limited evidentiary role explicit. Its expanded description was rechecked in the browser; the separate UNESCO heritage page again returned a human-verification interstitial and was not used as additional evidence.

The final documentary review removed the five unpublished records identified above and retained their source/artifact as discovery context. The retired IDs are not reused. The underlying historical claims retain their source attribution, `single_source` labels and unknown present-day status. Establishing that a text describes secrecy or initiation does not independently authenticate every event or practice it reports.
