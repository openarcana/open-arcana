## Mirror identity and dates

The Atefá site's uploader credit, Juan Perez, is not the compiler attribution. The uploader's identity and authority have not been verified. The extracted imprint lists editions in 2001, 2004 and 2007, a present-edition copyright in 2011, repeating 2012 footers and ISBN 9781105021794. `original_year=2001` records the stated first edition; `edition_year` remains empty because the inspected copy does not establish one unambiguous edition date. The online disclosure date also remains unknown.

The compiler's introduction describes a practitioner collection of manuscripts, comparison across houses and recognition of lineage variation. That is a source's account of its own process. Neither its claims of authenticity nor a retailer's matching author/title/ISBN constitute an independent audit of this mirror. The posting is not labelled an authenticated insider leak or a covert recording.

## Discovery record and limits

Queries covered `Mundurucu religion trumpets`, `Abakuá iniciación juramento archivo`, `Igbodu tratado pdf secretos Ifa`, `libretas Osha iniciación`, `Proyecto Orunmila ceremonias`, `Ceremonias de Atefá pdf`, `Ceremonias preliminares de Untefá pdf`, and `Bagre Goody Gandah full text pdf`. Routes included Berkeley's collection, the Biblioteca Digital Curt Nimuendajú index, Persée, practitioner websites, Lulu and retailer bibliographic records, PDFCoffee, Scribd, esoteric-library search results and publisher previews. Not every search hit became a source record.

- The Bagre results included publisher front matter, descriptions and reviews, rather than an inspected recitation or recording. This remains the unresolved lead recorded in the previous batch.
- A 20-page public Baba Eyiogbe booklet was captured from reglasparasanteros.com. Its file metadata and imprint were inspected as a related series lead; it is not counted here as an inspected initiation manual or a new restricted-content item.
- The esoteric-library directory request did not yield a usable manual listing. A search also surfaced an unrelated sweeping conspiracy allegation. Neither supplied a checked new restricted-practice record.
- No Anna's Archive, Z-Library, LibGen, Nexus/STC, IPFS or torrent payload was retrieved in this batch. The actual non-academic content inspected is the practitioner-attributed mirror identified above. Provider lists and promising titles are discovery routes, not evidence of successful retrieval.

The next substantive checks are original-edition collation for the mirror, access to its cited manuscripts where available, independent local evidence for the collected accounts, and dated evidence for any assessment of present practice. The catalogue does not infer politically motivated withholding from an access failure or an incomplete search.
